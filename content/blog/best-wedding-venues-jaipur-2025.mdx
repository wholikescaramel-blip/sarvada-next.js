# Best Wedding Venues in Jaipur for Every Budget (2025)

Jaipur is one of India's most sought-after wedding destinations — and for good reason. The Pink City offers a rare combination of royal architecture, warm weather, vibrant Rajasthani culture, and a wide range of venues that suit every kind of celebration. Whether you are planning an intimate ceremony for 100 guests or a grand multi-day affair for 1,000, Jaipur has a venue that fits.

At Sarvada Events, we have scouted and worked with venues across the city. This guide is based on real experience — not just listings.

## Luxury Palace Venues (₹10 Lakh and Above)

Jaipur's palace hotels are what most couples dream about when they think of a destination wedding in Rajasthan. These venues come with a heritage backdrop, expansive lawns, and world-class hospitality — but they require careful planning and early booking (at least 8–10 months in advance for peak season).

**Rambagh Palace** is the gold standard. Set in 47 acres of Mughal gardens, it offers multiple event spaces — the Rajput Room for intimate dinners, the Baradari for outdoor ceremonies, and the sprawling lawns for large receptions. Expect a premium price, but the experience justifies it.

**Chomu Palace** is an excellent alternative if you want the palace aesthetic at a comparatively accessible price. Located about 30 km from the city, it offers a stunning 300-year-old fortified palace setting and handles accommodation, catering, and decor in-house, which simplifies coordination.

**Fairmont Jaipur** is ideal for couples who want 5-star hotel infrastructure with a modern Rajasthani design sensibility. The ballrooms and outdoor lawns can handle large guest lists comfortably.

**Tip from us:** Palace venues look breathtaking in photographs but require a highly coordinated vendor team. If you are booking a palace venue, hire a dedicated wedding planner to manage logistics — the cost pays for itself.

## Mid-Range Venues (₹3–10 Lakh)

This is where Jaipur truly shines. There are dozens of beautifully designed banquet spaces and resort properties in this range, many of which offer the Rajasthani aesthetic without the palace price tag.

**Jai Mahal Palace** sits beautifully in this bracket — a heritage property with manicured lawns, available for both daytime and evening functions.

**Hotel Clarks Amer** is a popular choice for multi-day weddings because it offers both indoor banquet halls and outdoor lawn spaces, and the in-house catering team is experienced with large Indian wedding menus.

**Samode Haveli** (within the city) offers a boutique haveli setting — ideal for mehndi, sangeet, or engagement functions for smaller guest counts (up to 200).

**Tip from us:** In this budget range, always visit the venue in person before finalising. Photos on listing sites are often from the best possible lighting and setups. Check the actual size of the lawn, the kitchen facilities, and the parking capacity.

## Budget-Friendly Venues (Under ₹3 Lakh)

A beautiful wedding in Jaipur does not have to cost a fortune. There are several community halls, hotel banquet spaces, and farmhouses that offer genuine value.

**Birla Auditorium and surrounding grounds** suits larger gatherings with simpler requirements.

**Hotel Mansingh** and similar 3-star hotel banquet halls in Bani Park and Vaishali Nagar offer clean, manageable spaces with in-house catering and basic decor packages.

**Farmhouses on the outskirts of Jaipur** — particularly in areas like Jagatpura and Tonk Road — are increasingly popular for their open-air feel and flexible decoration possibilities. Rates are negotiable and vendors have more creative freedom.

**Tip from us:** Budget venues work best when you invest the savings into good decor and photography. A simple space transformed by a creative decor team photographs just as beautifully as an expensive venue.

## Things to Check Before Booking Any Venue

Regardless of budget, always confirm these before signing:

- **Catering policy:** Does the venue require you to use their in-house caterer, or can you bring your own? In-house catering is convenient but sometimes limits menu flexibility.
- **Generator backup:** Uninterrupted power is non-negotiable for an evening wedding.
- **Parking capacity:** For 500+ guests, inadequate parking creates chaos. Ask specifically — not just whether there is parking, but how many cars it holds.
- **Vendor access timing:** When can your decorators begin setup? Venues that allow access only 4–5 hours before the event limit how elaborate your decor can be.
- **Accommodation on-site or nearby:** For outstation guests, nearby accommodation makes a significant difference to their experience.
